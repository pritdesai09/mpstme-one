# 1. General Guidelines

- All students are expected to be professionally dressed and groomed to maintain decency and decorum of the "profession". For girl students, off shoulder tops/spaghetti tops/crop tops/transparent dress/shorts are strictly not allowed.
- The use of cell phones in class rooms/laboratories is strictly prohibited. Strict action will be taken against students using cell phones in the class room/laboratories.
- Misconduct of any sort from any student will be dealt with strictly. Any student resorting to any indecent, unruly behaviour or found causing disturbance, annoyance or raising voice for any reason which results in irritating or disturbing any other person, shall be considered as 'misconduct' and will be dealt with accordingly.
